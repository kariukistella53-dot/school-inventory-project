About Our Project

Project Overview

The School Inventory Management System is a computerized system designed to manage school resources and inventory records efficiently.

Problem Statement

Many schools experience challenges managing inventory using manual records. This can lead to misplaced records, poor tracking and difficulties monitoring available resources.

Project Objectives

  • To computerize school inventory records.
  • To monitor available school resources.
  • To reduce errors in inventory management.
  • To generate accurate inventory reports.
  • To identify items that require restocking.

Expected Benefits

Improved accuracy, accountability, efficiency and proper management of school resources.
